# REQ-005: event spec for review

> do people like the new trail guides?? can we track that

**Summary.** Too vague to spec yet. 'Do people like the new trail guides' doesn't name a user action or a measurable question. Guide engagement is already tracked. guide_viewed and guide_scrolled (25/50/75/100% depth) feed the trail_guide_engagement KPI, which measures the share of guide views scrolled past 75% and is owned by content, so Sam may be able to answer 'are people reading them' today with no new tracking. New events or properties are only needed if 'like' means something else, such as explicit feedback, product clicks or adds from a guide, or shares and saves. They are also needed if 'new' guides must be told apart from older ones. No events are proposed until Sam answers the questions below.

## Questions for the PM
- What would count as 'liking' a guide? Could you use the existing trail_guide_engagement KPI (share of guide views scrolled past 75%, from guide_viewed and guide_scrolled)? Or do you want an explicit signal, such as a 'Was this guide helpful? Yes/No' widget? If you want a widget, does one exist or is it planned for the guide page?
- How do we tell the 'new' trail guides apart from older ones? Options include a list of guide_ids, a publish or launch date, or a new template or version. Is the goal to compare new guides against older guides, or before against after the launch?
- Are there any actions after reading a guide that you'd treat as success? Examples are clicking a product featured in the guide, adding it to cart (product_added_to_cart.source has no 'guide' value today), or sharing or saving the guide.


**Privacy.** Nothing is captured yet. If Sam wants a feedback widget, use structured values, such as a helpful yes/no or a 1–5 rating, keyed by guide_id. Do not send free-text comments as event properties, because people often type names, emails or locations into them. If free text is needed, route it to a moderated feedback store rather than analytics. Share or save tracking should record only the channel (for example copy_link or email) and never the recipient's email or phone number.
